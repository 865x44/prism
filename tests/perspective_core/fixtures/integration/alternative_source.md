# Cognitive Load and Decision Bottlenecks in Complex Incident Response

## 1. Triage Dynamics under Uncertainty

During high-severity production outages, engineering responders face conflicting telemetry signals and escalating pressure from cross-functional stakeholders. Information overload frequently leads to cognitive tunnel vision, where responders fixate on the first plausible root cause rather than systematically exploring alternative failure hypotheses.

## 2. Coordination Costs and Escalation Paths

Incident command structures aim to decouple communication management from technical troubleshooting. However, handoffs between shifts or domain experts introduce semantic translation errors and lose diagnostic context accumulated during early triage phases.
