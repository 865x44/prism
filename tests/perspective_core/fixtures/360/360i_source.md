# Heterogeneous Semiconductor Chiplet Packaging and Thermal Management

High-performance computing processors increasingly adopt 2.5D and 3D heterogeneous chiplet integration on silicon interposers.
Localized thermal hotspots arising from non-uniform logic power dissipation create severe localized thermal gradients across chiplet stacks.
Thermal interface materials (TIM), integrated vapor chambers, and embedded microfluidic cooling channels distribute heat fluxes away from sensitive silicon dice.
Differential thermal expansion between dissimilar semiconductor materials induces mechanical stress and solder micro-bump interconnect fatigue over thermal cycles.
