# Organizational Knowledge Transfer in Engineering Firms

Complex engineering enterprises face continuity challenges when senior technical specialists retire or transition roles.
Standard knowledge preservation initiatives deploy centralized digital documentation repositories, process wikis, and structured exit interviews.
Tacit knowledge transfer occurs primarily through paired apprenticeship, joint failure postmortems, and informal peer communities of practice.
Systematic rotation programs and dual-track technical mentorship formalize the transmission of unwritten diagnostic heuristics.
