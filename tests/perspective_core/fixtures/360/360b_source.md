# Municipal Water Infrastructure and Urban Leakage Detection

Aging municipal water distribution networks experience substantial non-revenue water losses due to subterranean pipe fractures.
Traditional utility management deploys periodic manual acoustic survey teams and scheduled pipe replacement based on asset age.
Pressure management regimes utilize district metered areas (DMAs) and pressure reducing valves to mitigate pipe stress during off-peak hours.
Advanced utility telemetry integrates smart metering, acoustic noise loggers, and hydraulic modeling to localize anomalies.
