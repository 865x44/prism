# Satellite Constellation Post-Mission Disposal and Deorbit Operations

Mega-constellations in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) require reliable end-of-life disposal protocols to mitigate orbital debris collisions.
Standard post-mission disposal strategies utilize dedicated propulsive burns to lower perigee and achieve atmospheric reentry within 25 years.
Passive deorbit mechanisms include deployable drag sails, electrodynamic tethers, and solar radiation pressure orientation strategies.
Ground-based radar tracking networks catalog derelict spacecraft and compute close-approach conjunction probability assessments.
