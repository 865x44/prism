# Power Grid Frequency Regulation and Renewable Intermittency

Synchronous electrical grids require continuous second-by-second balance between power generation and aggregate consumer load to maintain system frequency.
Large-scale integration of inverter-based renewable resources (wind, solar PV) diminishes intrinsic physical rotational inertia from thermal turbogenerators.
Grid operators employ synthetic inertia algorithms, fast-responding battery energy storage systems (BESS), and automated demand-side response reserves.
Transmission constraints and localized rate of change of frequency (RoCoF) transients present emerging operational reliability risks.
