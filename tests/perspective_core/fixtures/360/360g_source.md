# Urban Bus Transit Signal Priority and Corridor Management

Municipal transit agencies implement Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridors to improve passenger throughput and schedule reliability.
Transit Signal Priority (TSP) systems use roadside optical sensors and GPS automatic vehicle location (AVL) to extend green light phases for approaching buses.
Uncoordinated signal preemption along dense arterials can induce severe cross-street private vehicular queuing and pedestrian delay.
Dynamic corridor management optimizes multi-modal green wave progression by weighing transit passenger volume against cross-traffic delay penalties.
