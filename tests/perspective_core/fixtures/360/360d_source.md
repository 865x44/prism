# Clinical Drug Development and Biomarker Stratification

Clinical translation of oncology therapeutics requires multi-phase human clinical trials to establish safety, dose tolerance, and efficacy.
Phase II trial protocols increasingly leverage predictive biomarker assays to enrich participant cohorts for likely responders.
Safety monitoring boards enforce predefined statistical stopping boundaries for adverse events and lack of efficacy.
Regulatory approval pathways evaluate overall survival endpoints and validated surrogate biomarkers under accelerated review frameworks.
